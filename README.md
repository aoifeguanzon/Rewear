# ReWear

🌱 Purpose & Vision
Help users shop sustainably by finding affordable second-hand fashion, connecting them with local clothing swaps, and building communities where they can share wardrobes and track their fashion impact.

Connects users to the world of secondhand fashion

🚀 Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
Upload product photo or link to search for secondhand dupes


Connect to local wardrobes for borrowing, swapping, and renting similar pieces

Social aspect where users can add friends and message them

🎯 Target Users
Age:
Younger teens (13–17): active on social media, trend-driven


Young adults (18–30): value sustainability, have spending power


Interests:
Fashion lovers focused on eco-friendly choices


Lifestyle:
Style-conscious, prefer secondhand, swapping, or renting


Location:
Urban/suburban — easier to build local clothing networks


Tech Comfort:
Familiar with web-based shopping and social platforms
